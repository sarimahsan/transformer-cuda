### 3. Concrete Solution: How to Match `torch.compile` ($95\text{ ms}$)

To drop our Pure CUDA step time from $121\text{ ms} \to \mathbf{95\text{ ms}}$:

1. **Eliminate the 18 `cudaMemcpyAsync` calls in `backward()`**:
   In [src/model.cu](file:///e:/CUDA/transformer-cuda/src/model.cu), replace the 3 `cudaMemcpyAsync` calls with zero-copy pointer aliases:
   ```cpp
   // Instead of copying d_block_out to d_ffn2_out:
   const float* d_ffn2_out = grads.d_block_out;
   ```
   This immediately saves $\sim 2.5 - 3.0\text{ ms}$ per step.

2. **Fuse `bias_backward` with `gelu_backward`**:
   Currently, we run `bias_backward` (reduction over $M$) and then `gelu_backward` (pointwise over $M \times N$). Fusing them into one kernel saves another $2 - 3\text{ ms}$.

3. **Optimize the Backward Softmax Kernel**:
   Apply vectorized `float4` loads and warp shuffles to `causal_softmax_bwd_kernel` in [src/kernels/attention.cu](file:///e:/CUDA/transformer-cuda/src/kernels/attention.cu), matching the $14.8\text{ ms}$ Triton kernel.
